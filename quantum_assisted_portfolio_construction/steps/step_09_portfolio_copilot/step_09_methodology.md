# Step 9 Methodology

The co-pilot is a deterministic presentation layer over validated portfolio outputs. It does not solve a new optimization problem.

The interface separates mandate-dependent recommendations:

- governance-first: strict-warning classical;
- base-objective efficiency: primary classical;
- hybrid-method demonstration: QAOA active-rebalance.

It reports allocation, material trades, risk and return trade-offs, hard-constraint status, soft warnings, scenario headroom, solver provenance, and exact-enumeration evidence.
