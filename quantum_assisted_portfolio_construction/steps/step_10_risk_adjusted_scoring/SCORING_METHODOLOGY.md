# Scoring Methodology

1. Disqualify portfolios with any hard-constraint breach.
2. Remove baselines and duplicate weight vectors.
3. Score the remaining candidates using net expected return divided by volatility, worst stress loss, and modeled drawdown.
4. Report a separate independently validated shortlist.
5. Test component-weight and benchmark-rate sensitivity.

The score is relative to the frozen candidate universe. It is not an externally standardized investment rating.
